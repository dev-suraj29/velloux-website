Studio philosophy

We do not ship templates with your logo on them

Velloux is a web design and development studio in Kharadi, Pune. We write every line by hand (layout, animation, copy structure and back-end logic) so your website loads fast, looks original and is built to be found.

Why Pune businesses choose a hand-coded website over a template

A hand-coded website is written specifically for your brand instead of assembled from a theme, a page builder or an unreviewed AI output. It loads faster, can be shaped around how your customers search, and can include real business logic such as billing, booking and dashboards.

It takes slightly longer than a template. It shows in speed, originality and search visibility.

Hand-written code

HTML, CSS and JavaScript built for your brief specifically, not assembled from a theme.

Search-ready by default

Clean heading structure, schema markup and fast Core Web Vitals baked in.

Business logic, not just design

GST-ready billing, booking flows and admin panels when the brief calls for them.

Every screen checked by hand

Real devices and real browsers, not just a resized window.

How a project runs

Our website development process in five steps

  1. 01

    Discover

    A short call to understand your business, customers and goals. We agree what the site must do and how success is measured.

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes and visual direction, reviewed with you before any code is written.

  3. 03

    Build

    Hand-coded front end and back end, tested on real phones and browsers as we go.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Speed, accessibility and SEO checks, structured data validation, then go-live with your sitemap submitted to Google Search Console.

  5. 05

    Grow

    Ongoing support, content updates and improvements as your business changes.
