Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Velloux collects through this website and how it is used. It is a plain-language template and should be reviewed by a qualified professional before publication.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we collect the name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address and project details you enter. The form opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message, so the information you send reaches us through WhatsApp.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and manage the project if you go ahead. We do not sell your information.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts, icons and scripts from third-party providers (such as Google Fonts and cdnjs), which may receive your IP address and browser information when the page loads. If analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us through the contact page.
