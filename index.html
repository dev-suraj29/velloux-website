Frequently asked questions
    
Website design questions, answered

  

    

      
        
What does Velloux do?

        
      
      
Velloux is a web design and development company in Kharadi, Pune. We design and hand-code websites, GST-ready billing software, e-commerce stores and technical SEO setups for clinics, IT service businesses, astrology and wellness brands and growing companies across India.

    

    

      
        
How much does a website cost in Pune?

        
      
      
A custom website with Velloux typically costs between ₹25,000 and ₹1,50,000. A focused single-page or small brand site sits at the lower end, while multi-page platforms with booking, billing or a custom backend sit higher. We quote against your actual scope, not a fixed template package.

    

    

      
        
How long does it take to build a website?

        
      
      
Most brand and business websites take 2 to 4 weeks from kickoff to launch. Platforms with custom logic, such as billing systems, booking flows or dashboards, usually take 4 to 8 weeks. The exact timeline is confirmed after the discovery call.

    

    

      
        
Is a hand-coded website better than WordPress or a page builder?

        
      
      
For speed, originality and custom features, usually yes. A hand-coded site loads only the code it needs, gives full control over SEO markup and can include custom logic such as billing or booking. WordPress and page builders can be quicker to start with, but they often depend on themes and plugins that add weight and limit the design.

    

    

      
        
Will my website show up on Google and in AI answers like ChatGPT?

        
      
      
We build the foundations that help: fast load times, semantic HTML, structured data (JSON-LD), answer-first content, a sitemap, crawler rules that allow search and AI bots, and an llms.txt file. No agency can guarantee rankings or AI citations, but these are the signals search engines and AI tools rely on.
