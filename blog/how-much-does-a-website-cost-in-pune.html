How much does a website cost in Pune? (2026 guide)

A plain-language guide to website pricing in Pune: what changes the cost, typical ranges and what to ask before you hire.

By Velloux · 5 November 2026

Key takeaways

  • A custom website in Pune typically costs between ₹25,000 and ₹1,50,000 with Velloux, depending on scope.
  • Page count matters less than custom features such as billing, booking or e-commerce.
  • Template sites look cheaper up front but often cost more in speed, limits and rework.
  • Budget for domain, hosting and optional maintenance as well as the build.

A custom website in Pune typically costs between ₹25,000 and ₹1,50,000. A focused single-page or small brand site sits at the lower end. A multi-page platform with booking, billing or a custom backend sits higher. The right number for you depends on what the site has to do.

What affects the cost of a website?

  • Scope and number of pages. More pages mean more design, writing and testing time.
  • Custom features. Billing, booking, dashboards, payment checkout and user accounts take the most development time.
  • Design depth. A fully custom design with motion takes longer than adapting a standard layout.
  • Content. Copywriting, photography and product data add work if you do not already have them.
  • SEO and performance work. Structured data, speed tuning and content structure add effort but pay back in visibility.

Template or custom: what are you actually paying for?

A template site reuses a ready-made layout, so the build is quicker and the invoice is smaller. The trade-off is that the design is shared with other sites, the code often carries extras you never use, and custom features are limited by what the template allows. A custom, hand-coded site costs more to start, but it loads only what it needs, can be shaped around how your customers search, and can grow with your business.

Costs beyond the build

Plan for a domain name, hosting and, if you want a developer on call, a monthly maintenance plan covering updates, monitoring and security. Ask any agency to list these separately so the full yearly cost is clear.

Questions to ask before you hire a web design company

  • Can I see live client websites that I can open on my phone?
  • Is the site hand-coded or built from a template or page builder?
  • What does the quote include, and what costs extra?
  • How is the site prepared for Google and AI search?
  • Who owns the files at handover, and what support exists after launch?
