E-Commerce Website Development questions

How much does an e-commerce website cost in Pune?

Cost depends on catalogue size, payment and shipping integrations and custom features. Velloux quotes each store against its scope; projects typically start from ₹25,000 for simple stores and rise with complexity.

Which payment gateways can you integrate?

We work with the payment gateways commonly used in India and confirm the best fit for your business during discovery.

Do you build custom stores or use Shopify?

We build custom, hand-coded stores. If a hosted platform such as Shopify is a better fit for your catalogue size or budget, we will tell you honestly during discovery.
