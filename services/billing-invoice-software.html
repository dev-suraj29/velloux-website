Common questions

GST-Ready Billing & Invoice Software questions

Is the billing software GST compliant?

It calculates GST automatically and prints the details a GST invoice needs, such as the tax breakup and your business details. Compliance rules depend on your business, so we configure the fields with you and recommend confirming your invoice format with your accountant.

Can I export invoices as PDF?

Yes. Invoices can be exported as PDF or printed directly from the browser.

Does the billing system need to be installed?

No. We build billing systems as web apps that run in the browser, so there is nothing to install and they work on desktop and mobile.
