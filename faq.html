Frequently asked questions

Website design questions, answered

Straight answers about cost, timelines, technology, GST billing and how we build websites that Google and AI tools can find, from a Pune web design company.

All questions

What does Velloux do?

Velloux is a web design and development company in Kharadi, Pune. We design and hand-code websites, GST-ready billing software, e-commerce stores and technical SEO setups for clinics, IT service businesses, astrology and wellness brands and growing companies across India.

How much does a website cost in Pune?

A custom website with Velloux typically costs between ₹25,000 and ₹1,50,000. A focused single-page or small brand site sits at the lower end, while multi-page platforms with booking, billing or a custom backend sit higher. We quote against your actual scope, not a fixed template package.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most brand and business websites take 2 to 4 weeks from kickoff to launch. Platforms with custom logic, such as billing systems, booking flows or dashboards, usually take 4 to 8 weeks. The exact timeline is confirmed after the discovery call.

Is a hand-coded website better than WordPress or a page builder?

For speed, originality and custom features, usually yes. A hand-coded site loads only the code it needs, gives full control over SEO markup and can include custom logic such as billing or booking. WordPress and page builders can be quicker to start with, but they often depend on themes and plugins that add weight and limit the design.

Can you build a GST-compliant billing system?

Yes. We have built billing and receipt software for an IT services business with automatic GST calculation, editable line items, logo embedding and PDF or print export. We adapt the fields and invoice format to your business.

Do you build websites for doctors and clinics?

Yes. Our dental clinic project includes WhatsApp appointment booking, a before-and-after smile slider and Google Business Profile details. We build clinic sites around the questions patients actually search for.

Will my website show up on Google and in AI answers like ChatGPT?

We build the foundations that help: fast load times, semantic HTML, structured data (JSON-LD), answer-first content, a sitemap, crawler rules that allow search and AI bots, and an llms.txt file. No agency can guarantee rankings or AI citations, but these are the signals search engines and AI tools rely on.

Do you work with businesses outside Pune?

Yes. We are based in Kharadi, Pune and meet local clients in person. We also work remotely with clients across India through video calls and WhatsApp.

What happens after the website launches?

We submit your sitemap to Google Search Console and hand the site over. You can then add an optional monthly maintenance plan covering content updates, monitoring, security patches and small new features.

How do I choose a web design company in Pune?

Look for live client work you can open on your phone, hand-written code rather than resold templates, clear pricing and timelines, and proof that the agency builds for search, such as structured data and fast load times. Ask who writes the code, what you receive at handover and what support exists after launch.

What technologies does Velloux use?

We write HTML, CSS and JavaScript by hand and use React or Next.js when a project needs an app-like experience. Animation is handled with GSAP and design with Figma. Every build is tested for performance, accessibility and SEO before launch.
